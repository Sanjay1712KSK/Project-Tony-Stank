# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors, users, and community members of the LLLMao project. We believe that diversity and inclusion strengthen our community and lead to better software.

This Code of Conduct outlines our expectations for all those who participate in our community, as well as the consequences for unacceptable behavior.

---

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

We expect all community members to:

- **Be Respectful** - Treat all individuals with respect, regardless of their background, experience level, identity, or opinions
- **Be Inclusive** - Welcome and support newcomers, beginners, and people from underrepresented groups
- **Be Collaborative** - Work together constructively, share knowledge, and help others succeed
- **Be Professional** - Maintain a professional tone in all interactions (issues, PRs, discussions, chat channels)
- **Be Constructive** - Provide thoughtful feedback that focuses on ideas and improvements, not personal criticism
- **Be Patient** - Remember that everyone has different skill levels and backgrounds
- **Be Honest** - Acknowledge mistakes gracefully and work toward solutions

### Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- **Harassment** - Any form of harassment, discrimination, or bullying based on race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, religion, disability, age, appearance, or any other protected characteristic
- **Abusive Language** - Offensive, derogatory, or inflammatory language
- **Intimidation** - Threatening or intimidating behavior toward any community member
- **Spam** - Unsolicited promotion, advertising, or repetitive off-topic content
- **Trolling** - Deliberately inflammatory or disruptive behavior intended to provoke conflict
- **Doxxing** - Publishing private personal information without consent
- **Sexual Harassment** - Unwanted sexual comments, advances, or requests for sexual favors
- **Violation of Privacy** - Sharing others' personal information without permission
- **Malicious Code** - Submitting code intentionally designed to harm, exploit, or compromise security
- **Intellectual Property Violations** - Infringing on others' copyrights, patents, or trademarks

## Enforcement

### Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it by:

1. **Direct Contact** - Email the project maintainers at [contact information]
2. **GitHub Issues** - Open a private security advisory if the issue is sensitive
3. **Anonymous Reporting** - Use community moderation tools if available

When reporting, please include:
- Description of the incident
- Who was involved
- When and where it occurred
- Any relevant links or context
- Your contact information (if you're comfortable sharing)

All reports will be taken seriously and handled confidentially.

### Consequences

Community members who violate this Code of Conduct may face:

1. **Warning** - A private message explaining the violation
2. **Temporary Restriction** - Temporary ban from commenting or contributing (24 hours to 7 days)
3. **Permanent Ban** - Permanent removal from the project and community spaces

The severity of the response will be proportional to the nature and severity of the violation.

### Appeal Process

If you believe action taken against you was unjust, you may appeal by:

1. Contacting the project maintainers with a written appeal
2. Explaining your perspective and any mitigating circumstances
3. Accepting the decision of the maintainers (appeals are reviewed once)

## Guidelines for Interactions

### For Contributors

- Before starting work, review open issues and pull requests to avoid duplication
- Use descriptive commit messages and PR titles
- Test your code before submitting
- Be open to feedback and iterate based on code review comments
- Acknowledge and credit others' contributions

### For Reviewers

- Review code objectively, focusing on the code, not the person
- Provide constructive feedback with suggestions for improvement
- Recognize and praise good work
- Be timely in your reviews
- Ask clarifying questions rather than making assumptions

### For Maintainers

- Respond promptly to issues and PRs
- Provide clear guidance and expectations
- Recognize contributor efforts
- Model the behavior you expect from the community
- Be transparent about decisions and project direction

---

## Conflict Resolution

If conflicts arise between community members:

1. **Direct Communication** - Try to resolve issues through respectful conversation
2. **Mediation** - Involve a neutral project maintainer if needed
3. **Documentation** - Keep a record of the resolution
4. **Follow-up** - Check that the resolution is working

## FAQ

**Q: What if I accidentally violate the Code of Conduct?**  
A: Most violations are honest mistakes. Acknowledge it, apologize, and do better. We believe in growth and learning.

**Q: Can the Code of Conduct change?**  
A: Yes. The Code of Conduct may be updated as the community evolves. Changes will be communicated clearly.

**Q: How is this different from GitHub's Community Guidelines?**  
A: This Code of Conduct is specific to our project community and may be stricter in some areas while complementing GitHub's platform guidelines.

**Q: What if I'm unsure about something?**  
A: Ask! Contact the maintainers or the community. It's better to ask than to unknowingly violate the Code of Conduct.
